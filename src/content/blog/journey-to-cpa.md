I’m officially starting my CPA test journey.

I bought the Becker course back in July, and somehow procrastinated all the way to June. Smh. I haven’t really studied any of it yet.

I’m shooting for the stars here, bro.

It’s basically the 4th quarter and I’m down 60. I’ve got 30 days to lock in because I’m testing for FAR on June 30th at 1 PM.

## The goal

Score above a 94.

The plan is simple:

- Spam practice questions
- Study all day
- For every question I miss or struggle with, write the underlying lesson on a note card
- Keep drilling until it sticks

If I can knock out the modules by mid-June, I’ll have real time to review before exam day. 

Let's see if future me can clutch it.
